4. Review after publishing

In Search Console, filter the Performance report to your page and inspect the queries it appears for. If you see impressions for a relevant question the page barely covers, check whether adding an answer would help the reader. Record the edit date and compare impressions and clicks across comparable periods. Changes in demand and competition can also affect those numbers.
